# Post-Mortem: Travel Rewards Upgrade (CMP-26Q2-UPG)

> Synthetic document for the Card Offer AI Assistant prototype. Metrics come from the synthetic dataset.

## Summary

An email upgrade offer to move affluent, high-spend customers to the Travel Elite card with the first-year
$95 annual fee waived. Response was strong and upgraders increased spend.

## Offer and Targeting

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Launch | 2026-04-06, window to 2026-06-30 |
| Offer | Product change to Travel Elite; $95 annual fee waived year 1; 3x points on travel |
| Segment | SEG-AFFLUENT-SPENDER v1.2 — income 150K+, FICO 700+, 6-month avg spend $2,500+, not on Travel Elite |
| Channel | Email |
| Holdout | 10% control |

## Results (Synthetic Sample)

| Metric | Treatment | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Contacted | 1,789 | 202 |
| Upgrade rate | 5.53% | 0.99% |
| Annualized spend lift per upgrader | ~$10,500 | — |

## Learnings

- Spend level was the strongest predictor of upgrade; customers spending $5K+ a month upgraded most often.
- The annual-fee disclosure (fee after year 1) must appear with the waiver claim. This campaign did so and
  had no fee-related complaints.
- Income is used only as an approved, certified attribute; no prohibited-basis attributes were used.
