# Balance Transfer Offer Terms and Disclosure Template (TPL-BT-STD-v4)

> Synthetic template for the Card Offer AI Assistant prototype. Not legal advice.

## 1. Headline Pattern (Approved)

> **0% intro APR for [PROMO_MONTHS] months on balance transfers** made within [WINDOW_DAYS] days.
> After that, your standard purchase APR of [GO_TO_APR]% applies. A balance transfer fee of [FEE_PCT]%
> (minimum $[FEE_MIN]) applies to each transfer.

Rules (Reg Z §1026.16(g) advertising of promotional rates):
- Use "intro" or "introductory" right next to the promo rate.
- Show the promo period and the go-to APR in the same view as the promo rate.
- The BT fee must appear in the same view as the promo rate in any headline offer.

## 2. Required Terms

| Field | Placeholder | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Promo APR | [PROMO_APR] | From approved grid (POL-PRC-004) |
| Promo period | [PROMO_MONTHS] | At least 6 months |
| Qualifying window | [WINDOW_DAYS] | Usually 60 days from offer date |
| BT fee | [FEE_PCT] / [FEE_MIN] | Stated plainly, never described as "no fee" |
| Go-to APR | [GO_TO_APR] | Account's current variable purchase APR |
| Payment allocation | fixed text | Amounts above the minimum go to highest-APR balance first |
| Credit limit | fixed text | Transfers count against the available credit limit |
| Interest on purchases | fixed text | Explain whether a grace period on purchases is lost while a BT balance is carried |

## 3. Disclosure Checklist

- [ ] Promo rate labeled "intro"/"introductory"
- [ ] Promo period and go-to APR in the same view
- [ ] BT fee and minimum shown with the offer
- [ ] No "no fee", "free" or contradictory claims (POL-MKT-001 §4)
- [ ] Payment-allocation statement included
- [ ] Prescreen opt-out notice included **if** the list used consumer report data (POL-FCRA-005)
- [ ] Unsubscribe (email) or STOP (SMS) instructions included
